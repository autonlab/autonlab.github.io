# Time Series Intelligence
Many high‑impact machine learning applications depend on understanding complex temporal data, including patient trajectories, sensor streams, financial signals, and large‑scale operational logs. The Auton Lab’s work in Time Series Intelligence focuses on developing robust, scalable, and adaptable machine learning methods for modeling, forecasting, and reasoning over time‑dependent data in real‑world environments.
A core objective of this research is to move beyond narrowly tailored forecasting models toward general‑purpose temporal representations that can support a wide range of downstream tasks with minimal retraining or supervision.
## Scalable Architectures for Long‑Horizon Forecasting
The lab developed [N‑HiTS](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12886>) (Neural Hierarchical Interpolation for Time Series), a deep learning architecture designed for efficient and accurate long‑horizon forecasting. By leveraging hierarchical decomposition and multi‑resolution learning, N‑HiTS enables scalable modeling of high‑frequency and long‑range temporal patterns, making it well suited for large datasets and operational forecasting problems.
## Foundation Models for Time Series
Inspired by the success of foundation models in natural language processing, the Auton Lab introduced [MOMENT](<https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03885>), the first open‑source Time Series Foundation Model (TSFM) designed to learn transferable representations across diverse temporal tasks, including forecasting, anomaly detection, classification, and imputation. MOMENT leverages large‑scale pretraining to enable rapid adaptation to new domains with limited task‑specific data.
Across MOMENT Small, Base, and Large, the model family has been downloaded over 3.78 million times since its release in 2024, reflecting widespread adoption across academia and industry and demonstrating the demand for reusable, foundation‑level models for time series intelligence.
## Applied Impact
Across this line of work, the lab emphasizes pragmatic deployment, generalization, and usability, aligning methodological advances with real‑world constraints. Auton Lab’s Time Series Intelligence research supports applications such as healthcare monitoring, early warning systems, predictive maintenance, and decision support in complex dynamical systems.
This area reinforces the lab’s broader mission to make machine learning systems reliable, interpretable, and actionable, particularly in settings where temporal structure is central to decision‑making.
